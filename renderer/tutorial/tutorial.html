第 3 步Step 3

  • 不想自动对齐：拖的时候按住 Don't want snapping? Hold while dragging
  • 想挪一点点：按方向键 ，按住 一次挪 10 像素Tiny moves: arrow keys , hold for 10 px steps
  • 拖文字会选中文字；要移动文字块，拖它的空白处或蓝框的边Dragging over text selects it. To move a text block, drag its empty area or the frame edge

第 5 步Step 5

选中文字：复制，或写批注Select text: copy it or add a note

按住鼠标从文字上拖过去，就选中了这段字。点旁边出现的 💬，写下修改意见。Drag across text to select it. Click the 💬 that appears and write your comment.

批注会保存在文件里。把文件交给 AI 时，让它“按批注修改”，它就知道改哪里。发给 AI 的记得是脱敏版本。Notes are saved inside the file. Hand it to AI and say "apply the notes" so it knows what to change. Only send AI the masked version.

  • 选中文字后点右键：复制 / 剪切 / 批注Right-click selected text: Copy / Cut / Note
  • 复制 · 剪切 · 批注 Copy · Cut · Note

  • 所有批注在顶部「批注」里，按页分组，可以标记处理状态All notes live under "Notes" at the top, grouped by slide, with a status

第 8 步Step 8

保存，然后放映Save, then present

按 保存。文档里用到的图片会合并进同一个文件，发给别人、换台电脑打开，图片都不会丢。Press to save. Images are packed into the same file, so nothing goes missing when you share it.

想保留原文件，就用「另存为」。To keep the original, use "Save As" .

按 全屏放映，用方向键或点击翻页，按 Esc 回到编辑。Press to present full screen. Use arrow keys or click to advance, Esc to come back.

🛡 编辑和保存都在你的电脑上，不会上传你的任何文件信息和内容。交给 AI 的是脱敏数据，真实数据只在这里填。Editing and saving happen on your computer, and nothing about your files is uploaded. AI gets the masked data; the real data is filled in only here.
