FAQ

常见问题Questions

为什么要先用脱敏数据（假数据）？Why use masked (fake) data first?

把公司数据、客户信息直接发给 AI，等于交给了第三方。换成脱敏数据再给 AI，版式照样做得好，真实内容只在你的电脑上出现。Sending company data or client details straight to an AI hands them to a third party. With masked data the AI designs the slides just as well, and the real content only ever appears on your computer.

脱敏数据怎么准备？How do I prepare masked data?

照着真实数据的样子换掉就行。比如“华东区营收 3,826 万”写成“A 区营收 1,000 万”，客户名写成“客户甲”。长度和格式尽量接近，填回真实数据时版式不容易乱。Follow the shape of the real data. For example, write "Region A revenue 10 million" instead of the real figure and "Client X" instead of the real name. Keep the length and format similar so the layout holds when you put the real data back.

我的文件会被上传吗？Is my file uploaded anywhere?

不会。打开、编辑、保存都在你的电脑上完成，不会上传你的任何文件信息和内容。软件只会匿名统计版本号和各功能的使用次数，用来改进产品。No. Opening, editing and saving all happen on your computer, and nothing about your files is uploaded. The app only sends anonymous counts of versions and feature use to help improve it.

会把我的原文件改坏吗？Will it break my original file?

不点保存就不会写入任何东西。保存时只把你改过的地方写回去，其余代码保持原样。每次保存还会留一个版本，改乱了可以退回。Nothing is written until you click Save. Saving only writes back what you changed and leaves the rest of the code as it was. Every save also keeps a version you can restore.

哪些幻灯片能打开？Which slides can it open?

常见 AI 工具生成的 HTML 幻灯片大多可以直接打开，包括 reveal.js、一页一页切换的、整页往下滚动的。完全靠脚本临时画出来的内容，可能改不了。Most HTML slides from common AI tools open as they are, including reveal.js, page-by-page and scrolling decks. Content drawn on the fly by scripts may not be editable.

要钱吗？Does it cost anything?

免费，代码开源（Apache 2.0 许可）。No. It is free and open source under the Apache License 2.0.

Mac 上提示“已损坏”或“无法打开”怎么办？macOS says the app is damaged or can't be opened

这是因为安装包还没有苹果签名。按 安装说明 执行一条命令就能打开，也可以把那页链接发给能操作电脑的 AI，让它帮你装。The package isn't signed by Apple yet. Run the one command in the install guide, or give that page to an AI assistant that can use your computer and let it install the app for you.
