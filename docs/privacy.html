SlideEase Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-19

Your files

SlideEase is a desktop app. Opening, editing and saving your slide files all happen on your own computer. SlideEase does not upload your files, file names, file paths, slide content, notes, or anything you change.

Data stored on your computer

To provide its features, SlideEase keeps a few things in its own data folder on your computer: the list of recently opened files, the edit history and save points of files you edited, and your settings (such as the interface language). This data never leaves your computer, and you can clear the edit history from inside the app.

Anonymous usage statistics

SlideEase sends a small amount of anonymous usage data to PostHog so we can see which versions and operating systems are in use, how many people use the app, and whether a new version is available. It contains only:

  • the app version, operating system type and version, CPU architecture, Electron version and interface language;
  • a random ID generated on your computer, not linked to your name, email or account;
  • the names of a few features when they are used (for example "save" or "preview").

It never contains your files, file names, paths, slide content or what you changed. Like any network request, the PostHog servers can see the IP address it comes from. We do not sell or share this data, and we do not use it for advertising.

SlideEase 隐私政策

更新日期：2026-11-19

你的文件

SlideEase 是一款桌面软件。打开、编辑、保存幻灯片文件都在你自己的电脑上完成。SlideEase 不会上传你的文件、文件名、文件路径、幻灯片内容、批注，以及你做过的任何修改。

保存在你电脑上的数据

为了提供功能，SlideEase 会在自己的数据目录里保存：最近打开的文件列表、你编辑过的文件的修改记录和保存点，以及你的设置（如界面语言）。这些数据不会离开你的电脑，修改记录可以在软件里清除。

匿名使用统计

SlideEase 会向 PostHog 发送少量匿名使用数据，用来了解使用的版本和操作系统、使用人数，以及检查是否有新版本。内容仅包括：

  • 软件版本、操作系统类型和版本号、CPU 架构、Electron 版本和界面语言；
  • 一个在你电脑上随机生成的编号，和你的姓名、邮箱、账号都没有关联；
  • 少数功能被使用时的名称（例如“保存”“预览”）。

其中不包含你的文件、文件名、路径、幻灯片内容或修改内容。和所有网络请求一样，PostHog 服务器能看到请求来自的 IP 地址。我们不会出售或共享这些数据，也不会用于广告。
